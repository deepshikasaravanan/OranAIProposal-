# 5.1 Program Management
The Contractor **shall** provide program management, reporting, and governance to support OCDAO initiatives.

## 5.1.1 Agile Cadence
The Contractor **shall** maintain a bi-weekly sprint cadence and report burndown metrics.

# 5.2 Infrastructure Architecture
The Contractor **shall** propose a standardized, cyber-hardened platform with reusable assets.

## 5.2.1 Tool Evaluation
The Contractor **shall** evaluate COTS and GOTS tools and provide adoption recommendations.

# 5.3 Data Engineering and Integration
The Contractor **shall** design and operate data pipelines and ingestion patterns that support ML adoption.

# 5.4 Strategy, Policy, and Responsible AI
The Contractor **shall** create governance artifacts and conduct RAI tabletop exercises.

# 5.5 Digital Talent Enablement
The Contractor **shall** develop a competency model and training playbooks.

# 5.6 Executive and PMO Support
The Contractor **shall** provide calendar, correspondence, and tasking support with service levels.
