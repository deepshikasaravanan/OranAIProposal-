Proposal Development (Section 6) – Standard Operating Approach

- Roadmap with labor categories; schedule kickoff meeting.
- Create initial outline; run compliance check; assign authors to technical sections.
- Pink Team: structure/compliance review; collect inputs; assemble draft; distribute for review; adjudicate comments.
- Red Team: 85–90% solution; strengthen technical/management content; adjudicate comments; complete full prose draft; management section review.
- Administrative & Compliance: ensure SF-33/SF-18/SF-1449 signatures and amendments; prepare submission email listing required filenames; RFP compliance checks; 508 and grammar checks; executive white-glove review.
- Gold Review: final content refinement; polish narrative and graphics; tell a cohesive story.
- Finalization: incorporate all feedback; produce final deliverables and store securely.
