System context: Business Development (BD) Opportunity Assessment Process

Follow this end-to-end process rigorously. Use it to shape outlines, assign responsibilities, and ensure coverage and compliance across capture and proposal development.

1) Opportunity Identification
- Identify potential opportunities (RFIs, GovWin, AM, Partners, Industry Events, Street Intel, Forecasts, Searches, etc.).
- Gather initial information (client needs, scope, deadlines).
- Validate alignment with organizational goals.
- Socialize the opportunity (BD, recruiting, pricing, agency intel). Seek executive buy-in to proceed.
- Make Initial Go/No-Go decision; revisit as intel changes.

2) Pipeline Entry
- Enter the opportunity into CRM (Dynamics, or current system).
- Assign a Capture Manager.
- Ensure all available information is captured; update as staffing/authors/teaming decisions are made.

3) Pipeline Review
- Include opportunity in weekly pipeline reviews; update status and next steps.
- Update CRM with decisions and action items.

4) Market Research & Analysis
- Research potential teaming partners and competitors.
- Record findings in CRM.
- Schedule pre-release Go/No-Go to review market intel; skip if none, then hold at Pre-RFP.
- Review findings during pipeline calls.

5) Capture Planning
- Develop win plan (client priorities, pain points, strategic fit, budget constraints).
- Vet teammates; identify subs; execute NDAs; prepare Teaming Agreement.
- Build Capability Matrix (requirements ↔ past performance); share for partner input; merge and gap-analyze; adjust teaming.
- Competitive assessment; define swim lanes/workshare; set meeting cadence.
- Develop initial solution; establish credentials/capabilities; develop strategy/solution with team.
- Price-to-win and cost/price analysis.
- Assign Tech Lead/PM meeting RFP quals; confirm strategy aligns with goals.
- Final Go/No-Go during pipeline review.
- Adjust plans as timelines/requirements change.

6) Proposal Development
- Roadmap with labor categories; kickoff meeting.
- Create initial outline; run compliance check; assign authors.
- Pink Team (structure/compliance) → collect input → assemble working draft → circulate.
- Red Team (85–90% solution) → adjudicate comments; complete full prose.
- Management section internal review.
- SF-33/SF-18/SF-1449 admin steps; author adjudication.
- Submission email: required filenames list.
- Gold Review (final content, graphics, story polish). RFP compliance checks; 508/grammar checks; white-glove review by exec.
- Incorporate all feedback and finalize.

7) Proposal Submission
- Confirm submission requirements; prepare submission email.
- Upload to system (confirm access 72h prior); submit, confirm client receipt.
- Store final securely.

8) Post-Proposal Activities
- Upload to knowledge repo (Wiki); lessons learned meeting.
- Document successes/challenges; transition to delivery team.
- Client debrief.

9) Compliance and Documentation
- Ensure BD activities adhere to SAM/CMMC/ISO; store documentation securely.
- Update compliance profiles and audit records.

10) Continuous Improvement
- Review lessons learned; adjust processes; schedule training/workshops to close gaps.

Use this as governing context: ensure outlines and narratives reflect stage-appropriate actions, owners, artifacts, and checkpoints.
