# Enhanced Content Generation Rules

## DOCUMENT QUALITY ENHANCEMENT

### Business Value Integration
- Every paragraph must start with a business value hook
- Include specific metrics: percentages, dollar amounts, timeframes
- Reference industry benchmarks and competitive analysis
- End each section with measurable outcomes

### Executive Decision Support
- Frame content for C-suite evaluation
- Include TCO (Total Cost of Ownership) considerations
- Address regulatory compliance and risk factors
- Highlight competitive differentiation

### Advanced Formatting Rules
- Use bullet points with quantified benefits
- Include risk/mitigation tables
- Add implementation timeline visuals
- Provide success metric dashboards

### Content Sophistication Levels
1. **Basic**: Generic technical approach
2. **Enhanced**: Business-focused with metrics
3. **Advanced**: Strategic with competitive analysis
4. **Executive**: Decision-ready with ROI justification

### Quality Checkpoints
- Does each paragraph advance the business case?
- Are risks identified with mitigation strategies?
- Is the implementation plan realistic and detailed?
- Are success metrics specific and measurable?
- Does content differentiate from competitors?

## CONTENT TEMPLATES

### Executive Summary Template
- Challenge/Opportunity (2-3 sentences)
- Our Solution Advantage (3-4 bullets with metrics)
- Business Impact (ROI, cost savings, risk reduction)
- Implementation Approach (timeline, milestones)
- Success Guarantee (metrics, warranties)

### Technical Approach Template
- Current State Analysis
- Proposed Solution Architecture
- Implementation Methodology
- Risk Mitigation Framework
- Quality Assurance Process
- Success Metrics and KPIs

### Management Approach Template
- Program Organization Structure
- Communication Strategy
- Risk Management Process
- Quality Control Framework
- Change Management Approach
- Performance Measurement System
